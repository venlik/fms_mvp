# Money Business Rules

## BR-MON-001

Money SHALL always have an associated Currency.

---

## BR-MON-002

Money arithmetic SHALL only occur between matching currencies.

---

## BR-MON-003

Money SHALL remain immutable.

---

## BR-MON-004

Currency conversion SHALL remain outside the scope of Version 1.

---

## BR-MON-005

The business SHALL operate using one configured Currency in Version 1.
