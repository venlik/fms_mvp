# Common Business Rules

## Purpose

Defines business rules applicable throughout the Finance Management System.

---

## BR-COM-001

Business Rules SHALL be enforced within the Domain layer.

---

## BR-COM-002

Expected business failures SHALL be represented using Domain Errors.

---

## BR-COM-003

Business operations SHALL preserve aggregate consistency.

---

## BR-COM-004

Aggregates SHALL only be modified through their Aggregate Root.
