# ADR-001

# Adopt Domain-Driven Design and Layered Architecture

## Status

Accepted

---

## Date

2026-09-10

---

## Context

The Finance Management System is expected to evolve beyond simple invoice generation into a modular business management platform.

The system will contain multiple business capabilities including Billing and future modules such as Inventory and Accounting.

Without a clear architectural approach, business rules risk becoming tightly coupled to user interface, persistence, and framework-specific code, increasing maintenance costs and reducing flexibility.

An architectural approach is therefore required that emphasizes business modeling, modularity, and separation of concerns.

---

## Decision

The Finance Management System SHALL adopt:

- Domain-Driven Design (DDD)
- Layered Architecture
- Modular Architecture

The architecture SHALL separate responsibilities into distinct layers including:

- User Interface
- Application
- Domain
- Persistence

The Domain SHALL represent the center of the system and remain independent of implementation technologies.

Business rules SHALL reside within the Domain.

Infrastructure concerns SHALL remain outside the Domain.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- Clear separation of responsibilities.
- Improved maintainability.
- Business logic independent of frameworks.
- Easier testing.
- Easier introduction of future business modules.
- Better scalability.

### Negative

- Increased initial design effort.
- More architectural concepts for new contributors to learn.
- Additional abstraction compared to small applications.

---

## Alternatives Considered

### Traditional Layered CRUD Architecture

Rejected.

While simpler initially, business logic tends to become distributed across multiple layers.

---

### Framework-Centric Architecture

Rejected.

Business concepts would become tightly coupled to implementation technologies.

---

### Transaction Script

Rejected.

Suitable for small applications but becomes difficult to maintain as business complexity grows.

---

## Notes

Subsequent architectural decisions SHALL conform to this architectural foundation unless explicitly superseded by another ADR.
