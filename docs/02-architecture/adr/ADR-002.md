# ADR-002

# Organize Code by Business Feature

## Status

Accepted

---

## Date

2026-09-09

---

## Context

The Finance Management System is expected to grow through the addition of new business capabilities.

Traditional package organization based on technical types (Entities, Repositories, Services, Controllers) separates closely related concepts into different locations.

As the project grows, navigation and maintenance become increasingly difficult.

A package organization aligned with business capabilities better reflects the Domain Model.

---

## Decision

The system SHALL organize source code primarily by business feature (module).

Examples include:

- Billing
- Inventory
- Accounting

Each business module SHALL own its related Domain, Application, Persistence, and User Interface concepts.

Within a module, related concepts SHALL remain physically close together.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- Higher cohesion.
- Easier navigation.
- Improved maintainability.
- Better alignment with the Domain.
- Simpler module evolution.
- Reduced cross-module coupling.

### Negative

- Similar technical components exist in multiple modules.
- Developers accustomed to type-based organization may require an adjustment period.

---

## Alternatives Considered

### Package by Layer Only

Example

Entities

Repositories

Services

Controllers

Rejected.

Related business concepts become physically separated.

---

### Package by Technical Type

Rejected.

The structure reflects implementation details rather than business capabilities.

---

### Hybrid Structure

Partially accepted.

Architectural layers remain separate at the project level.

Within each layer, organization SHALL follow business features.

---

## Notes

This decision complements ADR-001.

The package structure is intended to mirror the Domain rather than the implementation technology.
